# What's new (since 2.2.0)

- A new home: your apps in a row at the top, All, Notebooks and Folders with Sort, Search through every folder, and a + button for a new notebook, a folder or Share.
- Apps: full-screen programs from the Package Store. Show All lists them, with What's new and About at its foot. Two fingers swiped up from the bottom bring the home back.
- Package Store: browse, search and install apps and widgets. Each package shows its license before it installs.
- Quick settings: pull down from the top of any screen for brightness, warmth, rotation lock and auto brightness.
- A status bar on every screen, with the time, date, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and battery (Settings > Sleep & Clock > Status bar).
- Darker lines on the ruled, Cornell, graph, dot and pattern papers, as sharp as Seyes.
- What's new is a screen of its own, with every version's notes.
- Folders can have an icon or a picture (a folder's ... > Icon & picture...).
- Kindle notebooks: Settings > General > Import Kindle notebooks brings in the ink of your Scribe's own notebooks.
- Type into Grimoire's keyboards from a paired keyboard (with Settings > Shortcuts > Enable shortcuts on).
- Widgets and apps refresh only the part of the screen that changes.
- In Polish and Japanese, a few of the newest texts are still in English in this beta.
- Thanks to nebuff for the new home, Apps, the Package Store, folder icons, the Kindle notebook import and the Polish and Japanese translations.

If you want to support the future of this project, please consider donating to the Ko-fi link in the GitHub.
