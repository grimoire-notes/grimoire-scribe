# What's new (since 2.1.0)

- Files: browse the Kindle's storage from the Library (New > From a file... > Show Files in Library), with a Covers view of previews, and rename, delete or restore inside Grimoire's folders.
- Home: make any folder the place Grimoire opens at (Set as home).
- Templates and photos from a file: pick a picture anywhere on the Kindle, or drop a PDF in grimoire/templates for a template of each page, or use a page of another notebook.
- Fonts: Settings > Font > Add a font... picks one from the Kindle's own fonts or anywhere else.
- Send a PDF from the Share page on your phone or computer. Nothing is kept until you tap Import on the Kindle, and both screens show the same code.
- PDFs: web links come along as a card with a QR code for your phone, faint planners can be imported darker (Settings > System), and the same PDF again fills in any missing pages.
- KOReader: Open this book in Grimoire imports the PDF itself, in colour, and opens your page. Update the plugin in Settings > KOReader.
- Widgets: small tools over your notebook, like a calculator. Add one in Settings > Packages > Widgets.
- Faster zoom, and a smoother side panel.
- Auto brightness follows the room's light (Settings > Display), and Shift+[ and Shift+] change the pen size from a keyboard.
- A new Settings menu which improves the usability and readability of settings.
- Polish translation for Polish speakers using Grimoire.
- Safer: fonts are checked more strictly, and Grimoire's own files are read more carefully.
- Lasso: a selection made larger or smaller keeps its look, its lines and erasing scaled with it, and a lasso round only erased ink selects nothing.
- Thanks to nebuff for widgets, templates from a notebook, the new Settings menu, auto brightness, the Polish translation and the storage fixes, and to tibidibi for the faster zoom and side panel.

If you want to support the future of this project, please consider donating to the Ko-fi link in the GitHub.
